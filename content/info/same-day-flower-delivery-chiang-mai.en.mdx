Need flowers delivered today? Order online, choose today’s date at checkout, and pay securely by card. Same-day orders are accepted until **20:00 Thailand time**, subject to bouquet availability and delivery capacity. Delivery may take place after 20:00. If same-day delivery is not possible, we will contact you to confirm the next available option.

## How to order for same-day delivery

1. **Browse the catalog** and pick a bouquet.
2. **Add to cart** and select **today** as the delivery date.
3. **Pay online** through Stripe checkout.
4. We prepare the bouquet and deliver within our Chiang Mai service area when same-day is available for your timing and zone.

Questions before you order? Start in the catalog above — or contact us on LINE or WhatsApp if you need help choosing.
