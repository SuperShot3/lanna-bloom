## What the 20:00 cutoff means (Thailand time)

Same-day flower delivery in Chiang Mai follows **Thailand local time (ICT, UTC+7)**, not your home timezone.

- Orders are accepted until **20:00 Thailand time**.
- Working delivery hours are **08:00–20:00**.
- A bouquet ordered late in the window may still arrive after 20:00.
- After 20:00, same-day is closed. We confirm the next option, which may be the following day.

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

Order earlier than you think you need to. Afternoon heat in Chiang Mai is hard on freshly arranged flowers, and late orders compete with the remaining courier runs.

If the bouquet must land before a dinner, hotel check-in, or hospital visit, do not wait until 19:00. Same-day flower delivery in Chiang Mai is real during working hours — it is not a guarantee that every district still has a free run at the last hour.

Choose a bouquet that can travel. Tight, well-wrapped arrangements usually handle the van better than very loose, open styles on a hot day. We will not pretend every flower is in season every week; checkout shows what we can prepare today.
