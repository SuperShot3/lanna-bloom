## How does hotel flower delivery work in Koh Samui?

Most Samui orders go to hotels, resorts, or beach villas. Reception will usually accept flowers if they can match a **guest name as registered**.

A hotel name alone is not enough. Include:

<ArticleChecklistBlock title="Hotel delivery details" variant="essential">

- Guest full name **as spelled at check-in**
- Hotel name and a Google Maps pin to the lobby or main entrance
- Check-in date if the guest has not arrived yet
- Room or villa number if you know it—front desk hold is fine if you do not
- A working Thai phone number for the guest or a backup contact
- Surprise notes: hold at reception, do not call the guest, or deliver after check-in

</ArticleChecklistBlock>

Chaweng and Lamai hotels often sit close together. Pin the **lobby the guest is booked in**, not “Chaweng Beach” in general.

Airport and ferry arrivals cluster on Fridays and Saturdays. If the bouquet must be there for check-in, order ahead rather than assuming same-day will still be open.

## Flower delivery in Chaweng — เฉวง

Chaweng is the island’s main east-coast strip: hotels, shops, and beach condos. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

A lobby or house pin is more useful than “Chaweng Beach” as the only address—properties sit close together and names repeat.

For customers searching for **flower delivery in Koh Samui**, start with the [Koh Samui flower delivery catalog](/en/samui/flower-delivery).

## Flower delivery in Bo Phut and Fisherman's Village — บ่อผุด

Bo Phut and Fisherman's Village mix boutique hotels, restaurants, and beach houses on the north-east coast. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

The walking street is busy in the evening. Pin the **hotel or house entrance**, not the pier or the restaurant row.

## Flower delivery in Lamai — ละไม

Lamai is a slightly quieter east-coast beach south of Chaweng, with hotels, condos, and local sois. Typical fee is **from 300 THB**.

Hillside stays and split hotel buildings are common. Pin the **entrance the driver should use**, not the beach viewpoint.

## Flower delivery in Mae Nam — แม่น้ำ

Mae Nam is a north-coast beach with villas, small hotels, and longer stays. Typical fee is **from 300 THB**.

Many properties sit down sois off the beach road. Pin the gate, not the shoreline.

## Flower delivery in Bangrak and Choeng Mon — บางรัก เชิงมน

Bangrak and Choeng Mon cover the north-east tip, including stays near Samui Airport. Typical fee is **from 300 THB**.

Pin the hotel or village the recipient is actually in. “Near the airport” is not a drop-off point.

## Flower delivery in Lipa Noi and Taling Ngam — ลิปะน้อย ตลิ่งงาม

Lipa Noi and Taling Ngam are the quieter west and south-west coast: villas, small resorts, and sunset-facing stays. Typical fee is **from 350 THB**.

The run is longer than Chaweng. For a fixed date—check-in, anniversary, or a family visit—order ahead rather than assuming east-coast same-day timing.

## Flower delivery in Na Thon and Ang Thong — หน้าทอน อ่างทอง

Na Thon is the west-coast town and ferry pier; Ang Thong is the surrounding tambon on the island (not the marine park islands offshore). Typical fee is **from 350 THB**.

A pier name without a pin is easy to mismatch. Pin the hotel, house, or office the driver should enter.

## Flower delivery in Hua Thanon — หัวถนน

Hua Thanon sits south of Lamai: local streets, houses, and smaller stays. Typical fee is **from 350 THB**.

Gated houses often need a house number and a phone number the driver can call. A written soi name without a pin is easy to mismatch.

## Can I send flowers to a Koh Samui villa, condo, or gated village?

Yes, when the pin falls inside a listed area. Villas and villages succeed when the driver can pass security.

Include the project name, villa or unit number, and a reachable Thai number. If the recipient should not be called, name the villa manager, resort reception, or a neighbour who can accept the bouquet.

## Why is the delivery fee higher in some Samui areas?

A drop in Chaweng is not the same as a run to Taling Ngam or Na Thon. Distance around the island, hills, and the exact pin all affect the fee.

Showing those differences is clearer than advertising one low Samui-wide price and changing it later. Where possible, the applicable cost is visible before you complete the order. Zone rules also appear in our [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

If the recipient is checking into a Samui hotel, use the **name on the reservation**, not a nickname or an English spelling you hope reception will recognise. Busy Chaweng and Bo Phut front desks turn deliveries away when they cannot match a guest.

For Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Na Thon, and Hua Thanon, treat same-day as a bonus, not a plan. Order at least a day ahead when the date is fixed.

Do not leave flowers with an unmanned villa gate in the heat. Island sun on a tiled step for an hour does the bouquet no favours. Ask us to hold at reception or retry if nobody can receive them.

Koh Samui currently offers bouquet delivery. If you need a larger gift set of the kind we prepare in Chiang Mai, ask before you assume it is on the Samui catalog.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Entering only a hotel name without the guest’s registered name — reception may refuse the delivery.
- ✗ Pinning Chaweng Beach or Lamai Beach in general — the driver needs the actual hotel or villa entrance.
- ✗ Assuming we deliver to Koh Phangan or mainland Surat Thani — coverage is the listed Samui areas above.
- ✗ Ordering “today” from another time zone without checking Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7) — same-day follows local cutoff and capacity.
- ✗ Skipping a Thai phone number for a villa or village — security often will not open the gate without one.

## FAQ

### Can I send flowers to Koh Samui from overseas?

Yes. Choose a bouquet online, enter the Samui delivery details, and pay securely by card. You do not need to speak Thai or contact local shops yourself. After payment, we aim to confirm so you know the order is being handled. More tips: [send flowers to Thailand from abroad](/en/info/buy-flowers-online-chiang-mai-thailand).

### Can I order same-day flower delivery in Koh Samui?

Same-day depends on when you order (Thailand time), florist capacity, and the area. Chaweng and Bo Phut / Fisherman's Village are more often realistic when you order early enough; Lipa Noi, Taling Ngam, Na Thon, and Hua Thanon may need more notice. We show what is available for your pin instead of promising identical same-day service everywhere.

### What is the minimum flower delivery fee in Koh Samui?

Delivery fees start from **250 THB** in Chaweng and Bo Phut / Fisherman's Village. Lamai, Mae Nam, and Bangrak / Choeng Mon are typically from 300 THB. Lipa Noi / Taling Ngam, Na Thon / Ang Thong, and Hua Thanon are typically from 350 THB. Always confirm the fee shown for your pin on the [delivery areas map](/en/delivery-areas-thailand) or at checkout.

### Do you deliver flowers to hotels in Koh Samui before check-in?

Often yes, if reception will hold the bouquet for a registered guest. Include the check-in date, the guest’s registered name, and a note to leave the flowers at the front desk. If the hotel will not accept items before arrival, we will need a later window or a backup contact.

### Which details help Koh Samui flower delivery succeed?

Include the recipient’s full name, a working Thai phone number, the area if you know it, and a Google Maps pin. For hotels, add the registered guest name. For villas, condos, and villages, add the project name, unit, and gate instructions.

### Do you deliver flowers to every address in Koh Samui or Surat Thani?

No. We cover selected locations in the listed Samui areas above. Exact availability is confirmed by pin and area at ordering time. Koh Phangan, mainland Surat Thani, and other islands are not Samui coverage—message us with a Maps link before you pay if your pin is not on the list.
