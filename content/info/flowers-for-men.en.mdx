## Why are potted flowers often the better gift for men?

A cut bouquet asks him to find a vase, trim stems, and display something that can look romantic the moment it arrives. That is fine if you know he likes that gesture. If you are unsure, it can make him self-conscious.

A **flower in a pot** is different. It arrives ready to place. It reads as a plant, not a Valentine’s statement. In Chiang Mai we often send potted orchids to offices, condos, and hotel rooms because they sit cleanly on a desk or console and stay looking considered after the first day.

That is why we believe potted flowers — especially orchids — are usually the best **flowers for men**.

## What should you avoid sending?

There is no rule that men cannot receive roses. The problem is the *default* rose gift: a pink or red romantic wrap that looks like what someone would send a partner on a date night.

| Gift | How it often feels | Choose it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Potted orchid | A plant for the desk or home — easy to keep | You want a safe, lasting gift |
| Bright bouquet | Cheerful and straightforward | Birthday, new job, or thank-you |
| Classic red roses or pastel pink | Romantic, or “a gift for her” | You know he likes that look |

If roses are the right flower for *him*, choose a grounded, rustic style — not a soft pastel arrangement. When you are guessing, skip roses and send a pot.
