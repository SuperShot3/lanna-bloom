## When is Thai Mother’s Day?

Thai Mother’s Day falls on **12 August**, the birthday of Her Majesty Queen Sirikit. Families often give flowers, visit parents, and share meals. If you live overseas, the practical goal is simple: the bouquet should arrive on **11 or 12 August** Thailand time—not by your home clock.

Peak days are busy. Advance orders are calmer for everyone: you, the florist, and your mum.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Choosing the delivery date in your home time zone — Mum may receive flowers a day early or late in Thailand.
- ✗ Leaving only a building name without Mum’s full name and Thai phone — the courier may not reach anyone on arrival.
- ✗ Expecting a traditional jasmine garland from every online florist — garlands are often a market craft; soft bouquets and orchids are the reliable delivery options.
- ✗ Waiting until 12 August afternoon from overseas — Thailand may already be deep into the celebration day.
