## How does hotel flower delivery work in Phuket?

Most Phuket orders go to hotels, resorts, or serviced villas. Reception will usually accept flowers if they can match a **guest name as registered**.

A hotel name alone is not enough. Include:

<ArticleChecklistBlock title="Hotel delivery details" variant="essential">

- Guest full name **as spelled at check-in**
- Hotel name and a Google Maps pin to the lobby or main entrance
- Check-in date if the guest has not arrived yet
- Room or villa number if you know it—front desk hold is fine if you do not
- A working Thai phone number for the guest or a backup contact
- Surprise notes: hold at reception, do not call the guest, or deliver after check-in

</ArticleChecklistBlock>

Large west-coast resorts often have more than one lobby or a separate villa reception. Pin the **entrance the driver should use**, not the beach.

The same guest-name rule applies in our other cities; the Chiang Mai [hotel flower delivery guide](/en/info/flower-delivery-to-hotels-chiang-mai) walks through reception habits in more detail.

## Flower delivery in Phuket Town — เมืองภูเก็ต

Phuket Town is the island’s old centre: Sino-Portuguese streets, shops, condos, and local homes. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

A lobby or house pin is more useful than “Phuket Town” as the only address—sois sit close together and names repeat.

## Flower delivery in Kathu — กะทู้

Kathu sits inland between Town and Patong. It mixes housing, workplaces, and hillside addresses. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

Steep access and gated projects are common. Add the village or building name plus a number security can call.

## Flower delivery in Chalong — ฉลอง

Chalong is a frequent residential destination on the south-east of the island. You can order online for supported locations without calling a florist yourself.

Typical fee is **from 300 THB**. For a home or condo gift, include the project name, unit, and how the guard should reach the recipient.

## Flower delivery in Rawai and Nai Harn — ราไวย์ ในหาน

Rawai and Nai Harn are quieter southern beaches with villas, small hotels, and longer stays. Conditions still depend on the exact address.

Typical fee is **from 350 THB**. Many properties sit down narrow sois. Pin the gate, not the beach viewpoint.

## Flower delivery in Kata and Karon — กะตะ กะรน

Kata and Karon are a core west-coast hotel strip. Homes, workplaces, and hotels in the available area can be ordered online.

Typical fee is **from 350 THB**. The hill road between the two beaches is busy. Pin the **hotel entrance**, not “Kata Beach” in general.

## Flower delivery in Patong — ป่าตอง

Patong is dense: hotels, condos, and busy streets. We deliver to supported hotels and addresses here; we do not treat the beach or Bangla area as a vague drop-off point.

Typical fee is **from 350 THB**. If the stay is a surprise, ask reception to hold the bouquet rather than sending the driver into a crowded entrance without a name.

## Flower delivery in Kamala — กมลา

Kamala is a shorter west-coast beach north of Patong, with hotels, condos, and hillside villas. Typical fee is **from 400 THB**.

Gated drives and split-level access are common. A Maps pin plus the building name saves time on arrival.

## Flower delivery in Cherng Talay, Bang Tao, and Laguna — เชิงทะเล บางเทา ลากูน่า

This north-west stretch is villa and resort country: Laguna, Bang Tao beach, and Cherng Talay villages. Typical fee is **from 450 THB**.

Security often will not open without a project name, unit, and a phone number. Write the village the way it appears on the gate, not a nickname used in a booking chat.

For a fixed date—check-in, anniversary, or a dinner plan—order ahead rather than assuming Town same-day timing.

## Flower delivery in Thalang and Thep Krasattri — ถลาง เทพกระษัตรี

Thalang and Thep Krasattri cover more inland northern addresses, including Si Sunthon in the same checkout area. Typical fee is **from 450 THB**.

A written soi name without a pin is easy to mismatch. Add the tambon if you know it, then still drop a Maps pin.

## Flower delivery in Pa Khlok and remote east Phuket — ป่าคลอก

Pa Khlok and the remote east of the island are a longer run from the main west-coast hotel belt. Typical fee is **from 550 THB**.

Treat same-day here as a bonus, not a plan. If the date is fixed, order at least a day ahead.

## Flower delivery near Mai Khao, the airport, and Sakhu — ไม้ขาว

Mai Khao, the airport strip, and Sakhu are the far north-west: large resorts, airport hotels, and quieter beach road. Typical fee is **from 550 THB**.

Pin the **resort lobby the guest is booked in**. Several properties share the same stretch of beach road, and a nearby landmark is not enough.

## Can I send flowers to a Phuket villa, condo, or gated village?

Yes, when the pin falls inside a listed area. Villas and villages succeed when the driver can pass security.

Include the project name, villa or unit number, and a reachable Thai number. If the recipient should not be called, name the juristic office, villa manager, or a neighbour who can accept the bouquet.

## Why is the delivery fee higher in some Phuket areas?

A drop in Phuket Town is not the same as a run to Mai Khao or Pa Khlok. Distance across the island, traffic, and the exact pin all affect the fee.

Showing those differences is clearer than advertising one low Phuket-wide price and changing it later. Where possible, the applicable cost is visible before you complete the order. Zone rules also appear in our [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

If the recipient is checking into a Phuket hotel, use the **name on the reservation**, not a nickname or an English spelling you hope reception will recognise. Busy Patong and Kata front desks turn deliveries away when they cannot match a guest.

For Mai Khao, Pa Khlok, and Laguna / Bang Tao, treat same-day as a bonus. Order at least a day ahead when the date is fixed.

Do not leave flowers with an unmanned villa gate in the heat. Phuket sun on a tiled step for an hour does the bouquet no favours. Ask us to hold at reception or retry if nobody can receive them.

Phuket currently offers bouquet delivery. If you need a larger gift set of the kind we prepare in Chiang Mai, ask before you assume it is on the Phuket catalog.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Entering only a hotel name without the guest’s registered name — reception may refuse the delivery.
- ✗ Pinning Patong Beach or Kata Beach in general — the driver needs the actual hotel or villa entrance.
- ✗ Assuming we deliver to Phi Phi or other nearby islands — coverage is the listed Phuket areas above.
- ✗ Ordering “today” from another time zone without checking Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7) — same-day follows local cutoff and capacity.
- ✗ Skipping a Thai phone number for a villa or village — security often will not open the gate without one.

## FAQ

### Can I send flowers to Phuket from overseas?

Yes. Choose a bouquet online, enter the Phuket delivery details, and pay securely by card. You do not need to speak Thai or contact local shops yourself. After payment, we aim to confirm so you know the order is being handled. More tips: [send flowers to Thailand from abroad](/en/info/buy-flowers-online-chiang-mai-thailand).

### Can I order same-day flower delivery in Phuket?

Same-day depends on when you order (Thailand time), florist capacity, and the area. Phuket Town and Kathu are more often realistic when you order early enough; Mai Khao, Pa Khlok, and Laguna / Bang Tao may need more notice. We show what is available for your pin instead of promising identical same-day service everywhere.

### What is the minimum flower delivery fee in Phuket?

Delivery fees start from **250 THB** in Phuket Town and Kathu. Chalong is typically from 300 THB. Rawai / Nai Harn, Kata / Karon, and Patong are typically from 350 THB. Kamala, Cherng Talay / Bang Tao / Laguna, Thalang, Pa Khlok, and Mai Khao are higher. Always confirm the fee shown for your pin on the [delivery areas map](/en/delivery-areas-thailand) or at checkout.

### Do you deliver flowers to hotels in Phuket before check-in?

Often yes, if reception will hold the bouquet for a registered guest. Include the check-in date, the guest’s registered name, and a note to leave the flowers at the front desk. If the hotel will not accept items before arrival, we will need a later window or a backup contact.

### Which details help Phuket flower delivery succeed?

Include the recipient’s full name, a working Thai phone number, the area if you know it, and a Google Maps pin. For hotels, add the registered guest name. For villas, condos, and villages, add the project name, unit, and gate instructions.

### Do you deliver flowers to every address in Phuket, or to nearby islands?

No. We cover selected locations in the listed Phuket areas above. Exact availability is confirmed by pin and area at ordering time. Nearby islands such as Phi Phi are not Phuket coverage—message us with a Maps link before you pay if your pin is not on the list.
