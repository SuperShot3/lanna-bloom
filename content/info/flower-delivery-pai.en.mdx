## Which parts of Pai do you deliver to?

Pai is a mountain district, not one compact town. Pai township sits in Wiang Tai; the district also covers Wiang Nuea, Mae Na Toeng, Mae Hi, Thung Yao, Mueang Paeng, and Pong Sa. Two stays both listed as “Pai” can sit an hour of mountain road apart.

## How does hotel and resort flower delivery work in Pai?

Most Pai orders go to hotels, guesthouses, or mountain resorts. Reception will usually accept flowers if they can match a **guest name as registered**.

A hotel name alone is not enough. Include:

<ArticleChecklistBlock title="Hotel delivery details" variant="essential">

- Guest full name **as spelled at check-in**
- Hotel or resort’s complete name and a Google Maps pin to the lobby or gate the driver should use
- Check-in date if the guest has not arrived yet
- Room or villa number if you know it—front desk hold is fine if you do not
- A working Thai phone number for the guest or a backup contact
- Surprise notes: hold at reception, do not call the guest, or deliver after check-in

</ArticleChecklistBlock>

Pai Walking Street is a neighbourhood, not a complete address. Pin the **property entrance**, not the night market.

Many resorts sit outside the town centre, with a similar name to another property or a shuttle meeting point that is not the guest room. Pin the entrance the courier should reach.

Wiang Tai holds Pai township, Walking Street, Pai Hospital, and many central guesthouses. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

A lobby or house pin is more useful than “Pai Walking Street” as the only address—properties sit close together and names repeat.

## Flower delivery in Wiang Nuea — เวียงเหนือ

Wiang Nuea sits just north of the town centre: homes, small hotels, and quieter sois. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

## Flower delivery in Pong Sa — โป่งสา

Pong Sa is among the more remote Pai tambons. Typical fee is **from 450 THB**.

Mountain access can slow a run after rain. Send the Maps pin before you pay so we can confirm the route.

## Can I send flowers to a Pai villa, café, or gated stay?

Yes, when the pin falls inside a listed tambon. Villas and gated stays succeed when the driver can pass the gate.

Include the property name, villa or unit number, and a reachable Thai number. If the recipient should not be called, name the villa manager, resort reception, or a neighbour who can accept the bouquet.

Address habits that also help here: [how to give a clear delivery address](/en/info/flower-delivery-address-chiang-mai).

## Sending flowers to Pai Hospital

Pai Hospital is in Wiang Tai (111 Moo 1, Chaisongkram Road). Flowers may be delivered when the hospital and ward permit them.

Before you order, confirm that the recipient can receive flowers and provide:

- The patient’s full legal name
- Ward or room information if you know it
- A contact phone number
- Any hospital instructions you already have

Hospitals may restrict flowers in certain wards. If flowers are not suitable, ask us about another gift before you pay.

## Why is the delivery fee higher in some Pai tambons?

Pai town is compact; the district is not. A drop on Walking Street is not the same as a run into Mae Na Toeng, Mueang Paeng, or Pong Sa.

The fee reflects distance, mountain roads, and the time the courier needs to find a gated or hillside entrance. Showing those differences is clearer than advertising one Pai-wide price.

Checkout and the [delivery areas map](/en/delivery-areas-thailand) remain authoritative for that pin. Zone rules also appear in our [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

If the recipient is checking into a Pai hotel, use the **name on the reservation**, not a nickname. Small guesthouses and busy Walking Street front desks turn deliveries away when they cannot match a guest.

“Pai” on its own is not an address. Two mountain resorts with similar English names are common. A Maps pin to the gate saves more phone calls than a long written description.

Same-day is available across listed tambons, but it still follows **Thailand time (UTC+7)**, flower stock, and road access. For a weekend, a public holiday, or a stay in Pong Sa or Mueang Paeng, order at least a day ahead when the date is fixed.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Entering only a hotel name without the guest’s registered name — reception may refuse the delivery.
- ✗ Using “Pai Walking Street” as the complete address — the driver needs the actual property entrance.
- ✗ Pinning a viewpoint, canyon, or shuttle office instead of the guest room or lobby.
- ✗ Assuming Mae Hong Son town is included — coverage is listed Pai tambons only.
- ✗ Ordering “today” from another time zone without checking Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7).
- ✗ Skipping a Thai phone number for a villa or mountain resort — the courier often needs someone who can open a gate.
