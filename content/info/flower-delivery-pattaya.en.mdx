## How does hotel flower delivery work in Pattaya?

Most Pattaya orders go to hotels, resorts, or serviced apartments along the bay. Reception will usually accept flowers if they can match a **guest name as registered**.

A hotel name alone is not enough. Include:

<ArticleChecklistBlock title="Hotel delivery details" variant="essential">

- Guest full name **as spelled at check-in**
- Hotel name and a Google Maps pin to the lobby or main entrance
- Check-in date if the guest has not arrived yet
- Room number if you know it—front desk hold is fine if you do not
- A working Thai phone number for the guest or a backup contact
- Surprise notes: hold at reception, do not call the guest, or deliver after check-in

</ArticleChecklistBlock>

## Flower delivery in Central Pattaya — พัทยากลาง

Central Pattaya is the core beach and hotel strip. Homes, workplaces, and hotels in the available area can be ordered online.

Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. A lobby pin is more useful than “Beach Road” as the only address—several properties sit close together.

For customers searching for **flower delivery in Central Pattaya**, start with the [Pattaya flower delivery catalog](/en/pattaya/flower-delivery).

## Flower delivery in North Pattaya, Naklua, and Wongamat — พัทยาเหนือ นาเกลือ วงศ์อมาตย์

North Pattaya, Naklua, and Wongamat mix hotels, condos, and quieter beachfront stays. Conditions still depend on the exact address.

Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. If the building uses a side entrance or a separate drop-off, put that in the driver notes.

## Flower delivery in South Pattaya and the Walking Street area — พัทยาใต้

South Pattaya and the Walking Street area are busy, especially in the evening. We deliver to supported hotels and addresses here; we do not treat the nightlife strip as a vague drop-off point.

Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. Pin the **hotel or building**, not a nearby landmark. If the stay is a surprise, ask reception to hold the bouquet rather than sending the driver into a crowded entrance without a name.

## Flower delivery in Pratumnak — พระตำหนัก

Pratumnak sits between South Pattaya and Jomtien, with hotels and condos on the hill and along the coast. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**.

Steep access roads and gated buildings are common. A Maps pin plus the building name saves time on arrival.

## Flower delivery in Jomtien — จอมเทียน

Jomtien is a frequent destination for longer stays, beach condos, and family hotels. You can order online for supported locations without calling a florist yourself.

Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. For a birthday or welcome gift at a condo, include the building name, unit, and how security should reach the recipient.

## Flower delivery in Na Jomtien — นาจอมเทียน

Na Jomtien is farther south than Jomtien. Coverage is still available for supported pins, but the run is longer.

Typical fee is **from 350 THB**. For a fixed date—check-in, anniversary, or a dinner plan—order ahead rather than assuming city-centre same-day timing.

## Flower delivery in East Pattaya and Nong Prue — พัทยาตะวันออก หนองปรือ

East Pattaya and Nong Prue are more residential: housing estates, condos, and workplaces inland from the beach. Typical fee is **from 350 THB**.

Gated villages often need a village name, house number, and a phone number the guard can call. A written soi name without a pin is easy to mismatch.

## Can I send flowers to a Pattaya condo or gated village?

Yes, when the pin falls inside a supported zone. Condos and villages succeed when the driver can pass security.

Include the project name, building or house number, unit, and a reachable Thai number. If the recipient should not be called, name the juristic office or a neighbour who can accept the bouquet.

Address habits that also help here: [how to give a clear delivery address](/en/info/flower-delivery-address-chiang-mai).

## Why is the delivery fee higher in some Pattaya areas?

A drop on Beach Road is not the same as a run to Nong Prue or Na Jomtien. Distance, traffic, and the exact pin all affect the fee.

Showing those differences is clearer than advertising one low Pattaya-wide price and changing it later. Where possible, the applicable cost is visible before you complete the order. Zone rules also appear in our [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

If the recipient is checking into a Pattaya hotel, use the **name on the reservation**, not a nickname or English spelling you hope reception will recognise. Front desks in busy beach hotels turn deliveries away when they cannot match a guest.

For Na Jomtien and East Pattaya / Nong Prue, treat same-day as a bonus, not a plan. Order at least a day ahead when the date is fixed.

Do not leave flowers with an unmanned gate in the heat. If nobody can receive them, ask us to hold at reception or retry—sitting in the sun for an hour does the bouquet no favours.

Pattaya currently offers bouquet delivery. If you need a larger gift set of the kind we prepare in Chiang Mai, ask before you assume it is on the Pattaya catalog.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Entering only a hotel name without the guest’s registered name — reception may refuse the delivery.
- ✗ Pinning Walking Street or Beach Road in general — the driver needs the actual hotel or condo entrance.
- ✗ Assuming we deliver across all of Chon Buri — coverage is the Pattaya zones above, not Bang Saen, Si Racha, or nearby islands.
- ✗ Ordering “today” from another time zone without checking Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7) — same-day follows local cutoff and capacity.
- ✗ Skipping a Thai phone number for a condo or village — security often will not open the gate without one.

## FAQ

### Can I send flowers to Pattaya from overseas?

Yes. Choose a bouquet online, enter the Pattaya delivery details, and pay securely by card. You do not need to speak Thai or contact local shops yourself. After payment, we aim to confirm so you know the order is being handled. More tips: [send flowers to Thailand from abroad](/en/info/buy-flowers-online-chiang-mai-thailand).

### Can I order same-day flower delivery in Pattaya?

Same-day depends on when you order (Thailand time), florist capacity, and the zone. Central beach areas are more often realistic when you order early enough; Na Jomtien and East Pattaya / Nong Prue may need more notice. We show what is available for your pin instead of promising identical same-day service everywhere.

### What is the minimum flower delivery fee in Pattaya?

Delivery fees start from **250 THB** in Central Pattaya, North Pattaya / Naklua / Wongamat, South Pattaya, Pratumnak, and Jomtien. Na Jomtien and East Pattaya / Nong Prue are typically **from 350 THB**. Always confirm the fee shown for your pin on the [delivery areas map](/en/delivery-areas-thailand) or at checkout.

### Do you deliver flowers to hotels in Pattaya before check-in?

Often yes, if reception will hold the bouquet for a registered guest. Include the check-in date, the guest’s registered name, and a note to leave the flowers at the front desk. If the hotel will not accept items before arrival, we will need a later window or a backup contact.

### Which details help Pattaya flower delivery succeed?

Include the recipient’s full name, a working Thai phone number, the zone if you know it, and a Google Maps pin. For hotels, add the registered guest name. For condos and villages, add the project name, unit, and gate instructions.

### Do you deliver flowers to every address in Pattaya or Chon Buri?

No. We cover selected locations in the Pattaya zones listed above. Exact availability is confirmed by pin and zone at ordering time. If your area is not listed—such as farther Chon Buri towns or nearby islands—message us with a Maps link before you pay.
