## Why birthday timing matters more from abroad

Birthday gifts are date-sensitive. Same-day may be possible in **Chiang Mai** when you order early enough in Thailand time — see [same-day flower delivery](/en/info/same-day-flower-delivery-chiang-mai). From most overseas time zones, **order one day ahead** so the bouquet can arrive on the morning or afternoon you intend.

Availability, fees, and same-day options depend on the destination city. Chiang Mai has the fullest catalogue; selected destinations across Thailand offer bouquet delivery — check [Chiang Mai delivery areas & fees](/en/delivery-areas-chiang-mai).

## What makes a birthday order go smoothly

Focus on birthday-specific details:

- **Delivery date** chosen in Thailand time (ICT), not your home clock
- **Recipient name and Thai phone** (or hotel reception for a surprise)
- A short **birthday message** for the card
- Flexibility if a stem needs a **substitution** (same style and value)

Payment, address tips, Maps pins, and “recipient does not answer” rules are covered on the [general abroad guide](/en/info/buy-flowers-online-chiang-mai-thailand) and in the [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## Birthday surprises

Many overseas birthday orders are surprises. Say so in the order notes. You can keep the sender name off the card if you wish. We still need a reachable contact — hotel reception, condo office, or a trusted local number — so delivery can complete on the day.

Hotel stays are common for birthday trips; see [flower delivery to hotels in Chiang Mai](/en/info/flower-delivery-to-hotels-chiang-mai).
