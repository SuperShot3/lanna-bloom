- paying without a Thai bank account
- giving a **clear delivery address** for a hotel, condo, or home
- understanding **Chiang Mai time** for same-day options
- keeping a surprise delivery discreet

## International payment

Checkout accepts widely used **credit and debit cards** via Stripe. Where your device and bank support them, **Apple Pay** and **Google Pay** may also appear. PromptPay may show for eligible THB payments in a Thai banking app — overseas buyers usually pay by card.

You do not need a Thai bank account to complete the order.

## Recipient contact, hotels, and condos

Delivery succeeds when the courier can reach someone on arrival. Include:

- the **recipient’s full name** (as used at the hotel or condo)
- a **working Thai phone number** for the recipient or a local contact
- **hotel name + room** (if known), or condo/building name and unit
- a **Google Maps pin** when the entrance is hard to describe
- **surprise notes** (for example: do not call the recipient; coordinate with reception)

For hotel stays, read [flower delivery to hotels in Chiang Mai](/en/info/flower-delivery-to-hotels-chiang-mai). For address tips, see [how to provide a Chiang Mai delivery address](/en/info/flower-delivery-address-chiang-mai).

## Time zones and same-day delivery

Same-day options follow **Chiang Mai local time**, not your home timezone. Order earlier in the Chiang Mai day for the best chance — see [same-day flower delivery in Chiang Mai](/en/info/same-day-flower-delivery-chiang-mai) and the [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy) for cutoff and service hours.

If you are several hours ahead or behind Thailand, plan the delivery date carefully so the gift arrives when you intend.

## Surprise deliveries

Many overseas orders are surprises. In the order notes, say if the recipient should not be told in advance. We still need a reachable phone for the hotel front desk, condo office, or a trusted local contact so delivery can complete smoothly.
