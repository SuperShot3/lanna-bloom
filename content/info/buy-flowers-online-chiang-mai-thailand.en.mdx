Delivery succeeds when the courier can reach someone on arrival. Include:

- the **recipient’s full name** (as used at the hotel, condo, or office)
- a **working Thai phone number** for the recipient or a trusted local contact
- the **building type**: hotel, villa, condominium, hospital, office, or home
- **hotel name + room** (if known), condo/villa name and unit, ward/room for hospitals, or company and floor for offices
- a **Google Maps pin** when the entrance is hard to describe
- preferred **delivery date** (and a note if timing is sensitive)
- **surprise notes** (for example: do not call the recipient; coordinate with reception)

## Ordering from your country

The steps above are the same worldwide. Use these notes only for **timing**:

- **Singapore** — about one hour behind Thailand. Same calendar day is often fine; still respect Thailand cutoffs.
- **United Arab Emirates (Dubai)** — about three hours behind. For morning Thailand delivery, order the previous evening Thailand time.
- **Hong Kong** — same zone as Thailand. Still use Thailand cutoffs, not “end of your day.”
- **United Kingdom** — about six to seven hours behind. Order the previous day for reliable same-day Thailand delivery.
- **Belgium, Switzerland, France** — similar to the UK (Central European time). Plan the delivery date in Thailand time.
- **United States** — large time lag. Choose the delivery date carefully in Thailand time; order at least one day ahead when possible.
- **Australia** — ahead of Thailand. Evening in Australia can already be the next morning or afternoon in Thailand — avoid assuming “today” matches.

Payment, recipient details, and delivery confirmation work the same way from every country: card checkout, clear address or Maps pin, and a reachable Thai contact.
