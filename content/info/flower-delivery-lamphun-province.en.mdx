## Why does flower delivery cost more in some parts of Lamphun?

Lamphun is a province, not one city. A drop in central Mueang Lamphun is not the same as a run deep south or off the main routes.

The fee can reflect distance, which florist fulfills the order, transport needs, and the exact pin. Showing those differences is clearer than advertising an unrealistically low province-wide fee and adjusting later.

Where possible, the applicable cost is visible before you complete the order. Full zone rules also appear in our [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

If the recipient is in Li or Thung Hua Chang and the date matters, order at least a day ahead. Distant amphoes give us less room to recover if a pin is vague or the recipient’s phone is unreachable.

For Mueang Lamphun, Pa Sang, Ban Thi, and Wiang Nong Long, same-day is more often realistic when you order early enough—but still confirm the options shown for that pin rather than assuming city-centre timing.

A clear Google Maps pin almost always saves more stress than a longer written address.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Assuming one delivery fee for all of Lamphun — amphoes differ; check the map or checkout for your pin.
- ✗ Entering only a village name without a Maps pin — rural routes are hard to match from text alone.
- ✗ Waiting until the morning of a fixed date for a distant amphoe — advance ordering is safer for Li and Thung Hua Chang.
- ✗ Skipping a local phone number for the recipient — drivers need a reachable contact on arrival.

## FAQ

### Can I send flowers to Lamphun from overseas?

Yes. Choose flowers online, enter the Lamphun delivery details, and pay securely by card. You do not need to speak Thai or contact local shops yourself. After payment, we aim to confirm so you know the order is being handled. More tips: [send flowers to Thailand from abroad](/en/info/buy-flowers-online-chiang-mai-thailand).

### Can I order same-day flower delivery in Lamphun?

Same-day depends on the amphoe, when you order, and florist capacity. Some locations have faster options than distant ones. We show what is available for your destination instead of claiming identical same-day service everywhere. For distant amphoes or important dates, order in advance.

### What is the minimum flower delivery fee in Lamphun?

Delivery fees start from **250 THB** in central Mueang Lamphun. Other amphoes are typically higher (for example from 300 THB in Pa Sang, Ban Thi, and Wiang Nong Long, up to from 550 THB in Thung Hua Chang). Always confirm the fee shown for your pin on the [delivery areas map](/en/delivery-areas-thailand) or at checkout.

### What if I cannot find my Lamphun area on the map?

Contact us before ordering. We may still arrange delivery or confirm a fee manually. A Google Maps location is the most useful detail for unfamiliar or remote destinations.

### Which details help Lamphun flower delivery succeed?

Include the recipient’s full name, a working Thai phone number, amphoe if known, and a Google Maps pin. For hotels, hospitals, or workplaces, add the registered guest or patient name and any reception instructions. Address tips that also apply here: [clear delivery address guidance](/en/info/flower-delivery-address-chiang-mai).

### Do you deliver flowers to every address in Lamphun?

We cover selected locations across the eight amphoes when they fall within our current network. Exact availability is confirmed by pin and zone at ordering time. If your area is not listed, message us with a Maps link before you pay.
