## How does hotel flower delivery work in Hua Hin?

Most Hua Hin orders go to hotels, resorts, or beach condos. Reception will usually accept flowers if they can match a **guest name as registered**.

A hotel name alone is not enough. Include:

<ArticleChecklistBlock title="Hotel delivery details" variant="essential">

- Guest full name **as spelled at check-in**
- Hotel name and a Google Maps pin to the lobby or main entrance
- Check-in date if the guest has not arrived yet
- Room number if you know it—front desk hold is fine if you do not
- A working Thai phone number for the guest or a backup contact
- Surprise notes: hold at reception, do not call the guest, or deliver after check-in

</ArticleChecklistBlock>

Beach-road hotels often sit close together. Pin the **lobby the guest is booked in**, not “Hua Hin Beach” in general.

Friday and Saturday arrivals from Bangkok are common. If the bouquet must be there for check-in, order ahead rather than assuming same-day will still be open.

## Flower delivery near Bo Fai and Hua Hin Airport — บ่อฝาย

Bo Fai and the airport area sit north of the main beach strip. Airport hotels, housing, and quieter roads are typical here.

Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. Pin the hotel or village the recipient is actually in. “Near the airport” is not a drop-off point.

## Flower delivery in Hua Don — หัวดอน

Hua Don is a beach stretch in the southern Nong Kae area. Coverage is available for supported pins; the run is still local, not a long inland trip.

Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. Many stays sit down sois off the beach road. Pin the gate, not the shoreline.

## Flower delivery in Hin Lek Fai — หินเหล็กไฟ

Hin Lek Fai is inland from the hotel strip: housing, villages, and workplaces. Typical fee is **from 300 THB**.

Gated projects often need a village name, house number, and a phone number the guard can call. A written soi name without a pin is easy to mismatch.

## Flower delivery in Thap Tai — ทับใต้

Thap Tai is farther inland than the beach zones. Coverage is still available for supported pins, but the run is longer.

Typical fee is **from 350 THB**. For a fixed date—check-in, anniversary, or a family visit—order ahead rather than assuming beach-strip same-day timing.

## Can I send flowers to a Hua Hin condo, villa, or gated village?

Yes, when the pin falls inside a listed area. Condos and villages succeed when the driver can pass security.

Include the project name, building or house number, unit, and a reachable Thai number. If the recipient should not be called, name the juristic office, villa manager, or a neighbour who can accept the bouquet.

Address habits that also help here: [how to give a clear delivery address](/en/info/flower-delivery-address-chiang-mai).

## Why is the delivery fee higher in some Hua Hin areas?

A drop on the beach road is not the same as a run to Hin Lek Fai or Thap Tai. Distance, traffic, and the exact pin all affect the fee.

Showing those differences is clearer than advertising one low Hua Hin-wide price and changing it later. Where possible, the applicable cost is visible before you complete the order. Zone rules also appear in our [delivery policy](/en/info/delivery-policy).

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

If the recipient is checking into a Hua Hin hotel, use the **name on the reservation**, not a nickname or an English spelling you hope reception will recognise. Busy beach-road front desks turn deliveries away when they cannot match a guest.

For Hin Lek Fai and Thap Tai, treat same-day as a bonus, not a plan. Order at least a day ahead when the date is fixed.

Do not leave flowers with an unmanned condo gate in the heat. Gulf-coast sun on a tiled step for an hour does the bouquet no favours. Ask us to hold at reception or retry if nobody can receive them.

Hua Hin currently offers bouquet delivery. If you need a larger gift set of the kind we prepare in Chiang Mai, ask before you assume it is on the Hua Hin catalog.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Entering only a hotel name without the guest’s registered name — reception may refuse the delivery.
- ✗ Pinning Hua Hin Beach or Khao Takiab in general — the driver needs the actual hotel or condo entrance.
- ✗ Assuming we deliver across all of Prachuap Khiri Khan — coverage is the Hua Hin areas above, not Pran Buri or Sam Roi Yot.
- ✗ Ordering “today” from another time zone without checking Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7) — same-day follows local cutoff and capacity.
- ✗ Skipping a Thai phone number for a condo or village — security often will not open the gate without one.

## FAQ

### Can I send flowers to Hua Hin from overseas?

Yes. Choose a bouquet online, enter the Hua Hin delivery details, and pay securely by card. You do not need to speak Thai or contact local shops yourself. After payment, we aim to confirm so you know the order is being handled. More tips: [send flowers to Thailand from abroad](/en/info/buy-flowers-online-chiang-mai-thailand).

### Can I order same-day flower delivery in Hua Hin?

Same-day depends on when you order (Thailand time), florist capacity, and the area. Hua Hin Center, Khao Takiab / Nong Kae, Bo Fai, and Hua Don are more often realistic when you order early enough; Hin Lek Fai and Thap Tai may need more notice. We show what is available for your pin instead of promising identical same-day service everywhere.

### What is the minimum flower delivery fee in Hua Hin?

Delivery fees start from **250 THB** in Hua Hin Center, Khao Takiab / Nong Kae, Bo Fai / the airport area, and Hua Don. Hin Lek Fai is typically from 300 THB. Thap Tai is typically from 350 THB. Always confirm the fee shown for your pin on the [delivery areas map](/en/delivery-areas-thailand) or at checkout.

### Do you deliver flowers to hotels in Hua Hin before check-in?

Often yes, if reception will hold the bouquet for a registered guest. Include the check-in date, the guest’s registered name, and a note to leave the flowers at the front desk. If the hotel will not accept items before arrival, we will need a later window or a backup contact.

### Which details help Hua Hin flower delivery succeed?

Include the recipient’s full name, a working Thai phone number, the area if you know it, and a Google Maps pin. For hotels, add the registered guest name. For condos, villas, and villages, add the project name, unit, and gate instructions.

### Do you deliver flowers to every address in Hua Hin or Prachuap Khiri Khan?

No. We cover selected locations in the listed Hua Hin areas above. Exact availability is confirmed by pin and area at ordering time. Pran Buri, Sam Roi Yot, and other Prachuap Khiri Khan districts are not Hua Hin coverage—message us with a Maps link before you pay if your pin is not on the list.
