## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

Do not buy the 62-inch bear because it looks impressive on screen. In our deliveries, the oversized toy is right for a home birthday or a planned romantic reveal. It is the wrong default for a Nimman hotel, a shared condo lobby, or a hospital desk.

For a hotel surprise, send flowers plus a compact teddy, and put the guest’s **registered name** on the order. Reception can hold a small toy. A bear as tall as a child is a logistics problem, not a nicer gift.

If you are unsure, choose the 28-inch or 33-inch teddy, or a plush dog, and spend the rest of the thought on the bouquet and the message card. The toy should support the flowers, not fight them for space.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Ordering the largest teddy for a hotel or office — reception may not have somewhere to keep it, and the guest has to carry it through public areas.
- ✗ Sending only a toy when the recipient expected flowers — say in the card if the plush is the main gift.
- ✗ Skipping the registered hotel name — staff cannot match a teddy to “the guest in 512” without a name.
- ✗ Assuming every city checkout accepts toys — some destinations are flowers-only; remove the toy before you pay if checkout asks you to.
- ✗ Forgetting a reachable Thai phone number at a gated village or condo — security often will not accept a bulky gift without a contact.
