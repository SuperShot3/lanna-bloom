## What do you need before ordering hospital flowers in Phuket?

Reception will not guess the patient. Have these details ready:

<ArticleChecklistBlock title="Hospital delivery details" variant="essential">

- Patient full name **as registered at the hospital**
- Hospital name (and building if the site has more than one)
- Ward / department, plus room number if you know it
- A working Thai phone number for the patient or a relative
- Preferred date and a daytime window
- A Google Maps pin to the **main entrance or lobby**, not “Phuket Town” in general
- Notes: hold at reception, do not call the patient, or ask a relative to collect

</ArticleChecklistBlock>

If you do not have the room number, send the registered name, hospital, ward if known, and a phone number. We coordinate the drop-off the hospital will allow.

## How does hospital flower delivery work in Phuket?

The driver still needs a pin and a name someone at the desk can match. From hospital deliveries we handle in Chiang Mai, the desk pattern is the same: staff check the registered patient name, then say where flowers may be left.

<ArticleStepsBlock>

1. Choose a compact bouquet from the [Phuket catalog](/en/phuket/flower-delivery).
2. Enter the patient’s registered name, hospital, ward or room if known, a Thai number, and a Maps pin to the entrance.
3. Confirm the delivery date and fee shown for that Phuket area.
4. Pay securely online (international cards welcome).
5. We prepare the flowers and follow the hospital’s drop-off point—lobby, reception, or nurse station.

</ArticleStepsBlock>

Do not assume the bouquet will go into the room. If a relative can collect it at the desk, say so in the order notes.

### Hospitals in Phuket Town

**Bangkok Hospital Phuket** (โรงพยาบาลกรุงเทพภูเก็ต) is on Hongyok Uthit Road in Talat Yai. That is inside our **Phuket Town / Talad Yai / Talad Nuea** area. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. Pin the main entrance, not a nearby shop.

**Vachira Phuket Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลวชิระภูเก็ต) is Phuket’s main public provincial hospital, on Yaowarat Road in Talat Yai. The site is large. A ward or building name plus a pin to the entrance you were given is more useful than “Vachira” alone.

**Mission Hospital Phuket** (โรงพยาบาลมิชชั่นภูเก็ต) is a private hospital on Thep Krasattri Road in Ratsada. Ratsada is part of our **Phuket Town** area. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. Pin the hospital gate on that road—do not confuse it with Thalang Hospital further north.

**Phuket PAO Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลองค์การบริหารส่วนจังหวัดภูเก็ต), also called Phuket City Hospital, is a public hospital in Talat Yai. Typical fee is **from 250 THB**. Pin the entrance, not a nearby municipal office.

Private desks still need the **registered patient name**. A nickname or a passport spelling that does not match the file can stop the drop-off.

### Bangkok Hospital Siriroj and Dibuk Hospital (Wichit)

**Bangkok Hospital Siriroj** (โรงพยาบาลกรุงเทพสิริโรจน์) is the other Bangkok Hospital Group site on the island—previously known as Phuket International Hospital / Siriroj International Hospital. It is in **Wichit**, which is **not** a named checkout area on our Phuket map.

**Dibuk Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลดีบุก) is a private hospital in the BDMS network, on Chao Fa West Road in Wichit. The same pin rule applies.

Drop the hospital pin at checkout. If it is not listed, send us the Maps link before you pay. Do not assume Town or Chalong pricing until the pin confirms.

### Chalong Hospital

**Chalong Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลฉลอง) is a public community hospital in tambon Chalong—not a Bangkok Hospital Group site. It sits in our **Chalong** delivery area. Typical fee is **from 300 THB**.

Pin the hospital entrance. Public hospitals can be busier at the desk. A relative’s phone number helps if staff will not take flowers to a ward.

### Patong Hospital

**Patong Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลป่าตอง) is a public hospital on Sainamyen Road in Patong. That is our **Patong** area. Typical fee is **from 350 THB**. Pin the hospital gate, not Patong Beach or Bangla.

### Thalang Hospital

**Thalang Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลถลาง) is a public hospital on Thep Krasattri Road in tambon Thep Krasattri, amphoe Thalang. That is our **Thalang / Thep Krasattri** area. Typical fee is **from 450 THB**. Treat same-day here as a bonus; order ahead when the date is fixed.

## Which flowers work better for a Phuket hospital delivery?

Choose for the **room and the desk**, not for a hotel lobby photo.

| Choice | Why it often works | When to skip it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compact mixed bouquet | Easy to hold at reception; reads as a get-well gift | If the family asked for a specific flower |
| Gentle pink roses | Familiar, usually lighter fragrance | If you need a clearly non-romantic gift |
| Sunflowers | Cheerful and informal | If the family wants a very small bedside bunch |
| Large rose count (e.g. 51 stems) | Makes a statement at home or a hotel | Often too big for a hospital room or nurse station |
| Strong-scented lilies | Beautiful in a villa or lobby | Some wards ask visitors not to bring heavy fragrance |

We do not invent a hospital’s flower policy. If a relative says the ward refuses flowers, believe them—send a card-only note or wait until discharge.

## A Chiang Mai florist's honest advice

Do not order the largest bouquet “so it feels more generous.” Hospital rooms and nurse stations have little spare surface. A compact mix is more likely to be accepted and actually used.

Skip strong-scented lilies unless a relative has confirmed the ward allows them. Roses, gerberas, and mixed seasonal flowers are the safer default when you are ordering from another city or country.

For **Town hospitals** (Bangkok Hospital Phuket, Vachira, Mission, PAO), same-day is more often realistic than a west-coast hotel run—still check the date shown at checkout. For **Chalong**, allow that fee and travel time. For **Patong Hospital**, use the Patong pin and fee, not “Kathu” in general. For **Thalang Hospital**, order ahead when the date is fixed. For **Siriroj** and **Dibuk**, settle the pin first; do not guess the zone.

If the patient is in intensive or restricted care, ask a relative before you pay. Many of those areas will not take flowers at all. In that case, deliver to a family member’s hotel or home in a listed Phuket area instead.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ Entering only the hospital name without the patient’s registered name — reception may refuse the delivery.
- ✗ Pinning “Phuket Town”, Chalong Circle, or Patong Beach instead of the hospital entrance — the driver needs the actual gate or lobby.
- ✗ Assuming the bouquet will go to the bedside — many hospitals stop at reception or a nurse station.
- ✗ Choosing a large romantic spray or strong lilies — harder to accept in a small ward.
- ✗ Treating Bangkok Hospital Siriroj or Dibuk Hospital as automatic Town coverage — Wichit is not a named map area; confirm the pin.
- ✗ Confusing Mission Hospital on Thep Krasattri Road in Ratsada with Thalang Hospital further north — they are different sites and different fees.
- ✗ Ordering “today” from another time zone without checking Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7).

## FAQ

### Can you deliver flowers to a hospital room in Phuket?

Sometimes, but often the hospital asks for drop-off at reception or a nurse station. Give ward, room, and a phone number so we can follow their procedure. We do not override hospital rules to reach a bedside.

### Do you deliver to Bangkok Hospital Phuket, Chalong Hospital, and Vachira Phuket Hospital?

Yes, when the pin is in a listed area. Bangkok Hospital Phuket, Vachira, Mission Hospital, and Phuket PAO Hospital are in our Phuket Town area (typical fee from 250 THB). Chalong Hospital is in Chalong (from 300 THB). Patong Hospital is in Patong (from 350 THB). Thalang Hospital is in Thalang / Thep Krasattri (from 450 THB). Bangkok Hospital Siriroj and Dibuk Hospital are in Wichit—message us with the Maps link if that pin is not listed.

### What if I do not know the room number?

That is fine. Send the patient’s full registered name, the hospital name, and a reachable number for the patient or a relative. We use that to agree the drop-off the desk will allow.

### Which flowers are suitable for hospital delivery in Phuket?

Compact mixed bouquets, gentle pink roses, and sunflowers are practical starting points. Avoid oversized arrangements. Treat strong-scented lilies as a maybe until a relative confirms the ward. Phuket orders are bouquet delivery—do not assume Chiang Mai gift add-ons are available.

### Can I order same-day flower delivery to a Phuket hospital?

Same-day depends on when you order (Thailand time), florist capacity, and the area. Town hospitals are more often realistic when you order early enough; still use the date shown at checkout. Chalong, Patong, and especially Thalang need their own travel time. See [flower delivery in Phuket](/en/info/flower-delivery-phuket) for area timing.

### Can I send hospital flowers to Phuket from overseas?

Yes. Choose a bouquet online, enter the hospital details, and pay by card. You do not need to speak Thai or call the hospital yourself. After payment, we aim to confirm so you know the order is being handled.
